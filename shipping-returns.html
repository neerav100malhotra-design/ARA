Shipping & Returns

We're here to help you shop with confidence. Please review our policies before placing an order.

Important: Delivery timelines, charges and return eligibility will be confirmed by ARA. Please contact us before sending any item back.

Order Processing

Orders are reviewed to confirm product availability before processing. ARA will contact you if an item is unavailable or additional confirmation is needed.

Shipping & Delivery

Delivery availability, estimated delivery time and shipping charges will be communicated when your order is confirmed. Delivery estimates may vary by destination and courier.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery orders are subject to order and product availability confirmation. Please ensure your phone number and delivery address are accurate.

Returns & Exchanges

Please contact ARA before returning or exchanging an item. Return eligibility, time limits and item-condition requirements must be confirmed with our team before a return is arranged.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your item arrives damaged or differs from what you ordered, contact ARA promptly with your order number and clear photographs of the item and packaging.

Refunds

If a refund is approved, ARA will explain the applicable refund method and expected processing time before proceeding.

Contact Us

For shipping, return or refund questions, use the contact details published on our official Contact page.
