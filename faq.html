Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to common questions about shopping with ARA.

1. How do I place an order?

Browse the Shop, select a product, add it to your cart and complete the checkout form. Your order is subject to product availability confirmation.

2. Is Cash on Delivery available?

Cash on Delivery is currently offered at checkout, subject to order confirmation and delivery availability.

3. When will my order be shipped?

ARA will confirm product availability and the estimated delivery timeline before processing your order.

4. How can I track my order?

Once tracking is available, ARA will share the applicable tracking details with you.

5. Can I return or exchange an item?

Return and exchange eligibility depends on ARA's policy. Please review our Shipping & Returns page and contact us before sending an item back.

6. What if my product arrives damaged?

Contact ARA promptly with your order number and clear photographs of the product and packaging.

7. How do I contact ARA?

Visit our Contact page for the available contact details.
